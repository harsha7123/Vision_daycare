      Runtime workflow (every processed frame)

      1. Grab frameRTSP / webcam / file, sampled to 10-15 fps, 15 s ring buffer
      2. DetectYOLO pose: persons + 17 keypoints. YOLO detect: cell phones
      3. TrackByteTrack gives stable IDs across frames and occlusions
      4. Classify roleAdult / child: classifier, height and proportions, then a per-track vote
      5. Associate phonePhone near a wrist and head pitched toward it. A phone on a table doesn't count
      6. Update stateZone by feet position, EMA-smoothed phone score, per-track timers
      7. Evaluate rulesR1-R6 thresholds with hysteresis and cooldowns
      8. Emit eventFace-blurred snapshot + clip, stored, then Telegram / FCM / webhook

      Privacy by design

      • All video is processed on the on-premise edge box; only events leave the building.
      • Faces are blurred on every snapshot; clips are for authorised admins only.
      • No facial recognition or identity profiling, only the roles adult / child.
      • Events are deleted automatically after the retention period (30 days by default).
      • A human reviews every alert. The system supports supervision; it doesn't decide discipline.
